playing at marbles with the other." The real discovery was, however, made by his 
guardian, friend, and benefactor, Colonel Herbert, of the Queen's Regiment. 
A t thirteen, he was a brilliant solo violinist. Enterin g the Roya l College of 
Musi c in 1890, three years later, he wo n an open scholarship for composition . His
